Of 490 features, 211 are done (43%), 120 are half done (24%) and 159 are left (32%). The count is higher than your list's line count because combined lines are split: "Email, Google, Apple and phone sign-in", for example, counts as four.
Done means it works for members today. Half means part of it exists, or it works in only one app (phone or website); the brackets say what's missing. Left means it isn't built yet. Each item was checked in the code, not just against a screen.
These seven look finished but don't fully work. They're the first fixes:
1. Banned members can't be unbanned.
2. There's no screen to see who you muted.
3. The weekly digest switch does nothing.
4. Event reminders only appear when someone opens the app.
5. Community invite, report-a-community and verify-a-community have no buttons.
6. The Explore language filter can't be used because every community is English.
7. Some moderator tools work in only one app.
